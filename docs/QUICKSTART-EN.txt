VOXLOOM — QUICK GUIDE

1. Extract the entire ZIP, for example onto your desktop.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click VoxLoom.exe.
3. Drop recordings into the window or click “Choose files”.
4. Choose the recording language: German, English or automatic.
5. Enable “Distinguish speakers” to get speaker labels.
   Select the known speaker count, e.g. 2 for an interview, or choose automatic.
6. Choose a results folder and click “Start transcription”.
7. When finished, click “Open results folder”.
   Every recording gets Transcript.docx (Word), TXT, SRT and JSON files.

No internet, account or installation is needed.
Keep VoxLoom.exe, _internal and models together in the same folder.
Your original recordings are not modified.
Switch the interface between English and German in the top-right corner.

Please listen to the recording and correct the Word document afterwards.
Automatic transcripts and speaker labels can contain mistakes;
* marks an uncertain speaker assignment. Overlapping voices are difficult.
Speakers receive neutral labels: Speaker 1, Speaker 2, etc. Replace these in Word if needed.

Long recordings can take longer than their duration on an ordinary computer.
“Cancel” stops the current process. Completed results are kept.
If a file fails: check that it plays correctly or save it as WAV/MP3.
If models are missing: extract the entire ZIP again.

VoxLoom 0.1.0 · Windows 10/11 x64 · recommended: 8 GB RAM
This preview is not digitally signed. Institutional computers may require IT approval.
Do not disable security protections.
